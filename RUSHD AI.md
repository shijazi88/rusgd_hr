# RUSHD AI — Website Design System

## 1. Overview

Rushd AI is an enterprise-grade AI platform focused on:
- AI agent orchestration
- Scalable AI solutions
- Business transformation

This website is a **high-trust landing experience**, not a product UI.

Design goals:
- Build credibility instantly
- Communicate complexity simply
- Balance innovation with enterprise trust

---

## 2. Design Style

Core feeling:
- Clean
- Strategic
- Confident
- Minimal but premium

Influence:
- Consulting firms (McKinsey style)
- SaaS leaders (Stripe style)
- AI-native branding

## 6. Core Components

### 6.1 Buttons

Primary Button:
- Background: teal
- Rounded: 10–12px
- Medium weight

Secondary Button:
- Outline style
- Subtle border

Ghost Button:
- Text only

---

### 6.2 Cards

Default Card:
- White or light background
- Soft shadow
- Rounded (12–16px)

Feature Card:
- Icon + title + short text

Metric Card:
- Big number + label

---

### 6.3 Feature Grid

- 3-column layout
- Equal spacing
- Each item = feature card

---

### 6.4 Sections

#### Hero Section
- Big headline
- Supporting text
- CTA button
- Visual diagram

---

#### Platform Section
- Grid of features (6 items)
- Short descriptions
- Category labels

---

#### Value Section
("Built Different")
- Cards explaining advantages

---

#### Outcomes Section (Dark Section)

- Dark gradient background
- Highlight metrics:
  - 60%+
  - Real-time
  - 100%
- High contrast text

---

#### Process Section

- Step-based layout (01 → 04)
- Each step in a card

---

#### Trust Section

- Logos / certifications
- Enterprise credibility

---

#### CTA Section

- Strong headline
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA

---

#### Footer

- Multi-column links
- Dark background
- Company info

---

## 7. Visual Style Rules

✔ Use whitespace generously  
✔ Keep sections clean and separated  
✔ Use teal only for actions  
✔ Use dark section for emphasis  
✔ Maintain strong hierarchy  

---

✖ No clutter  
✖ No heavy gradients everywhere  
✖ No random colors  
✖ No inconsistent spacing  

---

## 8. Interaction & Motion

- Smooth fade transitions
- Subtle hover effects
- Button hover → slight darken
- No aggressive animations

---

## 9. UX Principles

- Clarity first
- Trust is key
- Content is structured
- Every section answers a business question
